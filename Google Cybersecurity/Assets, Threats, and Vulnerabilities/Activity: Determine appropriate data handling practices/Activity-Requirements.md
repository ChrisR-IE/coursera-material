## Step 1: Overview  
The principle of least privilege is a core security control that protects information privacy. Its effectiveness decreases when too many users receive access to sensitive data. Data leaks often occur when information is shared without proper oversight.

---

## Incident Summary  
A customer success representative received access to a folder of internal documents from a manager. The folder contained files related to a new product offering, including customer analytics and marketing materials. The manager forgot to unshare the folder.

Later, the representative attempted to share a link to a single marketing file with a business partner but accidentally shared a link to the entire folder. The business partner accessed internal documents and posted the link publicly on social media.
